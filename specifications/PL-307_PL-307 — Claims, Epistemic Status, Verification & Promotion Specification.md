## 1. Purpose

PL-307 defines the state model governing Loom claims and establishes the distinction between:

1. workflow status;
2. epistemic status;
3. verification level; and
4. promotion stage.

These dimensions must not be collapsed into a single claim-status field.

PL-307 also defines:

* the relationship between evidence, observations, claims, and diagnostics;
* contradiction handling;
* promotion eligibility;
* demotion and correction;
* validator requirements;
* diagnostic constraints; and
* provenance requirements.

Schema compliance is **necessary but not sufficient** for research credibility.

A valid object is not necessarily a true object.

A claim becomes credible only through the underlying evidence, verification, provenance, review, and correction processes defined elsewhere in the Loom system.

---

# 2. Core Epistemic Rule

> **AI may propose. Evidence must establish. Humans may promote.**

AI-generated material is never authoritative merely because it was generated by a model.

The Loom system must preserve the distinction between:

**proposal → evidence → assessment → verification → governance decision**

No AI agent may independently promote its own output to the permanent Loom research record.

---

# 3. The Four Dimensions

Every claim MUST maintain four separate dimensions.

| Dimension            | Question answered                                             | Examples                                                      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `status`             | Where is the object in the workflow?                          | `proposed`, `under_review`, `completed`                       |
| `epistemic_status`   | What does the available evidence currently allow Loom to say? | `unknown`, `contested`, `supported`, `known`                  |
| `verification_level` | How deeply has the claim been checked?                        | `none`, `source_backed`, `cross_verified`, `primary_verified` |
| `promotion_stage`    | Where does the claim sit in Loom's governed research record?  | `candidate`, `supported`, `canonical`                         |

These fields answer different questions and must not substitute for one another.

---

# 4. Workflow Status

`status` describes operational progress only.

Allowed values:

### `proposed`

The claim has been created but has not completed verification.

### `under_review`

Verification, contradiction analysis, or human review is in progress.

### `completed`

The applicable verification and contradiction procedures have been completed.

`completed` does **not** imply that the claim is supported.

### `rejected`

The claim has been determined unsuitable for further advancement.

### `deprecated`

The claim was previously used but is no longer considered valid or current.

A deprecated claim MUST reference the applicable correction, replacement, or supersession record.

---

# 5. Epistemic Status

`epistemic_status` describes the substantive evidentiary condition of the claim.

Allowed values:

### `proposed`

A human or machine has suggested the claim.

No evidentiary conclusion has yet been reached.

### `unknown`

Available research does not permit Loom to determine whether the claim is supported or contradicted.

Unknown is a legitimate research result.

### `unresolved`

Relevant evidence exists, but the available evidence is inadequate, conflicting, incomplete, or otherwise insufficient to resolve the claim.

### `contested`

Credible evidence supports the claim while credible evidence also challenges it.

The contradiction MUST be recorded.

### `supported`

The available evidence supports the claim under the applicable Loom requirements.

Limitations may remain.

Supported does not mean absolute proof.

### `known`

A deliberately restricted status indicating that high-quality evidence establishes the claim under Loom's current standards.

`known` MUST NOT be used as an ordinary synonym for `supported`.

Unless otherwise specified by a domain-specific methodology, `known` requires:

* no unresolved contradiction;
* primary verification where an applicable primary source exists;
* human review;
* explicit scope limitations.

If these requirements cannot be satisfied, the claim MUST NOT be assigned `known`.

---

# 6. Verification Level

Verification level describes the depth of checking performed.

For claims, allowed values are:

```text
none
source_backed
cross_verified
primary_verified
```

### `none`

No substantive verification has occurred.

### `source_backed`

At least one retrieved source contains evidence that appears to support the claim.

A URL discovered through search is insufficient.

### `cross_verified`

The claim has been examined against multiple relevant and independent sources.

Independence MUST be evaluated rather than inferred from source count.

### `primary_verified`

The relevant primary documentation has been examined where an applicable primary source exists.

If no applicable primary source exists, the verification record MUST explicitly record:

```text
primary_applicability: not_applicable
```

`primary_verified` MUST NOT be claimed where applicable primary documentation has not been examined.

For artifacts, additional verification levels may include:

```text
editorially_verified
published
```

These describe artifact-level review and publication, not merely claim verification.

---

# 7. Source Retrieval Requirement

A source is considered **retrieved** only when Loom has obtained usable documentary content.

Examples include:

* a local document;
* an API response;
* an archive snapshot;
* OCR text derived from a retrieved document;
* a verified documentary excerpt.

The following do NOT constitute retrieval:

* a search result;
* an unvisited URL;
* an AI-generated citation;
* a reference to a document that Loom cannot access;
* a secondary description of an unavailable document.

A source that has only been discovered MUST NOT independently serve as evidence.

---

# 8. Source Independence

Source independence means independence **from the subject of the claim and from other purportedly corroborating sources**.

A source may be primary without being independent.

For example, a government agency reporting on its own program may constitute a primary source while also representing an interested institutional perspective.

Similarly, ten news articles reproducing the same wire report do not constitute ten independent sources.

Loom therefore evaluates:

* provenance;
* institutional relationship;
* shared underlying source;
* citation relationships;
* syndication;
* derivative reporting; and
* substantive independence.

Source count MUST NOT be treated as equivalent to evidentiary independence.

---

# 9. Evidence Object

An `EVD` object represents documentary evidence extracted from a retrieved source.

Evidence MUST preserve sufficient provenance to permit a reviewer to identify its origin.

At minimum:

```yaml
id: EVD-0001
type: evidence
source_id: SRC-0001
extracted_content: "..."
location: "page 14"
retrieved_at: 2026-09-08
document_hash: "sha256:..."
```

Evidence is not itself a claim.

The evidence record should represent what the source contains before Loom interprets what that information means.

---

# 10. Observation Object

`Observation` is an explicit research object.

It is a preliminary interpretation derived from evidence.

Observation is **not** a promotion stage.

The distinction is:

```text
Evidence → Observation → Claim
```

An observation may state:

> The available records indicate that warnings were present before the intervention.

The observation is an interpretation requiring further formulation and testing.

The claim may subsequently state:

> State officials received documented warnings concerning water quality before the emergency declaration.

The claim then enters Loom's verification system.

This prevents `Observation` from being simultaneously:

* an informal note;
* a research object; and
* a governance stage.

---

# 11. Promotion Stages

Promotion stages describe governance decisions regarding inclusion in the Loom research record.

The stages are:

```text
Candidate → Supported → Canonical
```

### `candidate`

The claim has sufficient basis to warrant continued research or inclusion as a provisional research object.

### `supported`

The claim has satisfied the applicable evidence and verification requirements.

### `canonical`

The claim has been explicitly admitted to the permanent Loom research record.

Promotion is a governance action.

Meeting verification conditions creates **eligibility** for promotion. It does not automatically promote an object.

The validator MUST therefore return:

```text
ELIGIBLE_FOR_PROMOTION
```

rather than:

```text
PROMOTED
```

when the necessary conditions are met but a governance decision has not occurred.

---

# 12. Promotion Eligibility

The basic relationship is:

```text
Epistemic assessment
        +
Verification requirements
        +
Provenance completeness
        +
Applicable review
        ↓
PROMOTION ELIGIBILITY
        ↓
Human governance decision
        ↓
Promotion
```

A claim MUST NOT become canonical solely because an automated validator determines that its evidence requirements have been met.

---

# 13. Promotion Matrix

| Epistemic status | Possible stage                                    | Constraint                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `proposed`       | `candidate`                                       | Provisional only                             |
| `unknown`        | `candidate`                                       | Cannot advance as supported knowledge        |
| `unresolved`     | `candidate`                                       | Must remain explicitly incomplete            |
| `contested`      | `candidate`, potentially `supported`              | Contradiction must be documented             |
| `supported`      | `candidate`, `supported`, potentially `canonical` | Requires applicable governance               |
| `known`          | `supported`, potentially `canonical`              | Restricted; requires additional requirements |

This matrix establishes **eligibility**, not automatic promotion.

# 14. Contradiction Handling

Contradiction MUST be represented structurally.

A claim MUST support:

```yaml
contradictory_evidence:
  - EVD-0011

contradiction_status: unresolved
contradiction_note: "..."
contradiction_resolution: null
```

Allowed values:

```text
none
documented
unresolved
resolved
```

### `none`

No credible contradictory evidence has been identified.

### `documented`

Contradictory evidence has been identified and recorded.

### `unresolved`

A substantive contradiction remains unresolved.

### `resolved`

The contradiction has been evaluated and a documented resolution has been reached.

`documented` MUST NOT be used to conceal unresolved disagreement.

---

# 15. Contradiction Constraints

If contradictory evidence exists:

* `epistemic_status: known` is invalid when the contradiction remains unresolved.
* `promotion_stage: canonical` is invalid when the contradiction remains unresolved.
* `contradiction_status` MUST accurately reflect the state of the conflict.
* Any resolution MUST identify the reasoning and evidence supporting the resolution.

A claim may remain `supported` despite contradictory evidence only where the contradiction has been explicitly assessed and does not overturn the claim under the applicable methodology.

# 16. Claim Schema

Minimum claim representation:

```yaml
id: CLM-0001
type: claim
statement: "..."
request_id: REQ-0001

status: under_review
epistemic_status: contested
verification_level: cross_verified
promotion_stage: candidate

related_evidence:
  - EVD-0001
  - EVD-0004

contradictory_evidence:
  - EVD-0011

contradiction_status: unresolved
contradiction_note: "State timeline provides an alternative explanation."
contradiction_resolution: null

scope:
  population: null
  geography: "Flint, Michigan"
  timeframe: null
  exclusions: []

provenance:
  proposed_by: "human"
  proposed_at: "2026-09-08T15:32:00Z"

public: false
created: "2026-09-08"
updated: "2026-09-08"
```

# 17. Scope Integrity

Every substantive claim MUST have an identifiable scope.

Scope MAY include:

* population;
* geography;
* timeframe;
* institution;
* event;
* measurement;
* exclusions;
* applicable conditions.

A claim MUST NOT become broader during the transition:

```text
Evidence → Observation → Claim → Diagnostic
```

without an explicit evidentiary basis for the expansion.

A diagnostic MUST NOT imply a broader causal or institutional conclusion than its supporting claims permit.

---

# 18. Provenance and Actors

Claims MUST preserve the identity or role of actors involved in their creation and review.

At minimum, provenance should distinguish:

```text
proposed_by
extracted_by
verified_by
reviewed_by
promoted_by
```

Actors may be:

```text
human
system
agent
model
```

Where applicable, machine provenance SHOULD include:

* system or agent identifier;
* model identifier;
* model version;
* prompt or instruction reference;
* timestamp;
* input object IDs.

The presence of machine provenance does not confer evidentiary authority.

---

# 19. Verification Events

Verification history MUST be append-only.

A verification event should record:

```yaml
event_id: EVT-0001
claim_id: CLM-0001
actor: human
action: contradiction_review
timestamp: "2026-09-08T15:32:00Z"

inputs:
  - EVD-0011

result: unresolved

reason: "Contradictory timeline remains materially relevant."
```

Existing verification events MUST NOT be silently rewritten when the claim changes.

Corrections and subsequent assessments MUST create new events.

---

# 20. Diagnostics

A `DIAG` object represents an analytical interpretation using Loom's ontology.

A diagnostic is not itself evidence.

A diagnostic may use claims but cannot upgrade them.

### Hard constraint

> **A diagnostic's epistemic status cannot be stronger than the weakest material supporting claim.**

Therefore, if a diagnostic depends upon a claim that is:

```text
unknown
unresolved
contested
```

the diagnostic MUST NOT be represented as unqualified `supported` or `known`.

The diagnostic MUST preserve the uncertainty of its underlying claims.

Diagnostics MUST NOT:

* invent ontology nodes silently;
* suppress contradictory evidence;
* diagnose from a single weak source where stronger evidence is required;
* manufacture causal certainty;
* promote their own conclusions;
* exceed the scope of their supporting claims.

If no existing Loom concept adequately describes the evidence, the diagnostic SHOULD record that limitation rather than silently creating a new category.

# 21. Invalid-State Validation

The Loom validator MUST identify epistemically inconsistent objects.

Examples include:

### Invalid: unresolved contradiction + known

```yaml
epistemic_status: known
contradiction_status: unresolved
```

Result:

```text
INVALID_STATE
RULE: EPISTEMIC-004
SEVERITY: ERROR
REMEDIATION: Resolve contradiction or downgrade epistemic status.
```

### Invalid: source-backed without retrieved evidence

```yaml
verification_level: source_backed
```

when no retrieved source or mapped evidence exists.

### Invalid: primary verification without applicable primary evidence

```yaml
verification_level: primary_verified
```

without qualifying primary-source evidence.

### Invalid: canonical unresolved claim

```yaml
promotion_stage: canonical
epistemic_status: unresolved
```

### Invalid: diagnostic exceeds supporting claim

```text
DIAG status > weakest supporting CLM status
```

The validator MUST reject the object or mark it ineligible.

---

# 22. Validator Rule Structure

Every validation error MUST include:

* rule code;
* severity;
* affected object;
* reason;
* required remediation;
* whether override is permitted.

Example:

```yaml
rule_code: EPISTEMIC-004
severity: error
object_id: CLM-0003
reason: "Claim declares known while contradictory evidence remains unresolved."
remediation: "Resolve contradiction or downgrade epistemic status."
override_allowed: true
```

---

# 23. Override and Correction

Validation rules may encounter legitimate exceptional circumstances.

An override MUST NOT silently bypass a rule.

Where an override is permitted, it MUST record:

```yaml
override:
  rule_code: EPISTEMIC-004
  authorized_by: "..."
  reason: "..."
  timestamp: "..."
  review_reference: "REV-..."
```

A correction object MUST be created where the override changes the public or permanent research record.

Overrides are governance events, not validator failures to be hidden.

---

# 24. Demotion

Promotion is reversible.

A claim may move:

```text
Candidate → Supported → Canonical
```

and later:

```text
Canonical → Contested
Canonical → Revised
Canonical → Deprecated
Canonical → Rejected
```

A demotion MUST preserve:

* previous state;
* new state;
* triggering evidence;
* reason;
* reviewer;
* timestamp;
* correction reference.

Canonical means admitted to the permanent research record.

It does not mean permanently immune from revision.

# 25. Claim State Examples

## Supported

```yaml
status: completed
epistemic_status: supported
verification_level: primary_verified
promotion_stage: supported

contradictory_evidence: []
contradiction_status: none
```

Interpretation:

The verification process is complete, primary documentation was applicable and examined, and the evidence currently supports the claim.

---

## Contested

```yaml
status: completed
epistemic_status: contested
verification_level: cross_verified
promotion_stage: candidate

contradictory_evidence:
  - EVD-0011

contradiction_status: unresolved
```

Interpretation:

The claim has meaningful supporting evidence but also meaningful contradictory evidence. It remains provisional.

---

## Unknown

```yaml
status: completed
epistemic_status: unknown
verification_level: cross_verified
promotion_stage: candidate
```

Interpretation:

Loom searched the available evidence but cannot responsibly determine the answer.

Unknown is an acceptable result.

# 26. Schema Compliance Is Not Research Credibility

PL-307 explicitly rejects the proposition that a formally valid object is necessarily a credible research finding.

Credibility additionally requires:

* real retrieved sources;
* authentic evidence extraction;
* appropriate source evaluation;
* meaningful contradiction search;
* accurate provenance;
* human review where required;
* transparent limitations;
* correction mechanisms; and
* adversarial testing.

The schema prevents certain forms of epistemic failure.

It does not prove that the underlying research is correct.

# 27. Machine Enforcement Requirement

The rules in this specification MUST eventually be represented in machine-readable schemas and validation tests.

Markdown documentation alone does not constitute enforcement.

Minimum implementation requirements:

```text
schemas/
    claim.schema.yaml
    evidence.schema.yaml
    observation.schema.yaml
    diagnostic.schema.yaml
    verification.schema.yaml
    provenance-event.schema.yaml
```

and:

```text
tests/
    fixtures/
    adversarial/
    results/
```

At minimum, the test suite MUST demonstrate that the validator rejects:

1. `known` + unresolved contradiction;
2. `canonical` + unresolved epistemic status;
3. `source_backed` without retrieved evidence;
4. `primary_verified` without applicable primary evidence;
5. diagnostic status stronger than supporting claims.

---

# 28. Adversarial Testing Is a Release Requirement

Adversarial testing is not a future final phase.

Every implementation of PL-307 MUST be tested against known failure conditions.

Tests MUST ask:

* Can an unretrieved source become evidence?
* Can derivative sources be falsely counted as independent?
* Can a contradictory source disappear from the final representation?
* Can an unsupported claim become canonical?
* Can a diagnostic exceed its evidence?
* Can the system manufacture certainty from insufficient evidence?
* Can a correction be made without destroying historical provenance?

A release that fails a critical epistemic test MUST NOT be labeled as satisfying PL-307.

# 29. Relationship to Other Loom Objects

The intended architecture is:

```text
REQUEST
   ↓
SOURCE
   ↓
EVIDENCE
   ↓
OBSERVATION
   ↓
CLAIM
   ↓
VERIFICATION
   ↓
PROMOTION
   ↓
ARTIFACT
```

Diagnostics operate on the verified research layer:

```text
CLAIMS + EVIDENCE
        ↓
    DIAGNOSTIC
```

The promotion ladder is independent of the evidentiary pipeline:

```text
Candidate → Supported → Canonical
```

This separation prevents the research object lifecycle from being confused with the evidentiary status of a claim.

# 30. Normative Summary

Loom MUST preserve the following distinctions:

**Workflow is not truth.**

**Verification depth is not epistemic status.**

**Epistemic status is not promotion.**

**Primary source is not automatically independent.**

**Source count is not corroboration.**

**Contradiction documentation is not contradiction resolution.**

**Schema validity is not research credibility.**

**AI agreement is not evidence.**

**Canonical is not infallible.**

And above all:

> **AI may propose. Evidence must establish. Humans may promote.**

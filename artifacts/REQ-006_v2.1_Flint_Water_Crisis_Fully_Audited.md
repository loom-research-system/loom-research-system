The v2.0 draft contained critical structural flaws masquerading as spec compliance:
1. **Phantom Evidence:** Cited `EVD-006-002` and `SRC-006-001` were undefined or misattributed.
2. **Independence Failure:** Attempted to use an MDEQ self-report as an independent source for an MDEQ action claim, and cited two evidence objects from the same source to claim `cross_verified`.
3. **Atomicity Violation:** Compound claims masked a lack of corroboration for individual assertions.
4. **Event Log Absence:** Promotion and verification were asserted via state changes, not logged governance actions, violating the anti-rubber-stamp mandate of §18.
5. **Scope Drift:** The diagnostic thesis claimed "active dismissal of information," but no supporting claim actually addressed information availability.

### Validation of v2.1 (Final)
The v2.1 artifact has successfully remediated all 7 failure points identified in the prior review:
1. `CLM-006-001a` now correctly cites `EVD-006-001` (Task Force) and `EVD-006-007` (EPA), satisfying the two-independent-evidence-object rule.
2. `CLM-006-004` is now backed by fully defined `EVD-006-005` and `EVD-006-006`.
3. `CLM-006-003` is correctly framed as a world claim with an `unknown` status, not a meta-claim.

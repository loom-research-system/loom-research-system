RESEARCH AREA · EROS

Executive Reversal Operating System.

EROS studies what makes executive policy commitments durable, reversible, or dependent on implementation chains that can be changed without changing the headline decision.

STATUS · IN DEVELOPMENT · NOT A COMPLETED VERIFIED SYSTEM

THE QUESTION

When an executive policy changes, what actually has to change underneath it?

Executive actions can look decisive while depending on regulations, appropriations, procurement, personnel, data systems, guidance, and intergovernmental cooperation. EROS maps those dependencies so reversibility can be studied as a structural property rather than inferred from political rhetoric.

The research area is a subproject of Loom, not a replacement for the Loom methodology.

02

HOW IT CONNECTS TO LOOM

EROS is a test of the same research discipline.

POLICY MECHANISM NODES

Executive orders, regulations, guidance, procurement, data, and personnel are treated as different mechanisms in an implementation chain.

DEPENDENCY CHAINS

The research asks which later decisions, authorities, resources, and institutional relationships must remain intact for an executive commitment to persist.

REVERSAL

Instead of asking only whether a policy was “reversed,” EROS asks what changed in the dependency chain and what remained structurally locked in.

TECHNICAL RECORD

The detailed EROS artifacts belong in the research repository and technical documentation. The public presentation deliberately gives the research question first and the implementation machinery second.

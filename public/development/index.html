06

NOT YET CLAIMED

What Loom is deliberately not pretending is finished.

Not autonomous.

Loom is not being presented as a fully operational autonomous research system.

Not automatically verified.

Automation cannot establish source truth merely because a pipeline completed successfully.

Not independently audited.

The complete research stack has not yet received an independent external audit.

07

BUILDING PRINCIPLE

Specification → implementation → artifact → adversarial test → public description.

A technical feature is not evidence. A validator passing is not proof that a claim is true. The system exists to preserve, test, and expose the research record.

The website is the public presentation layer — not a substitute for the underlying research.

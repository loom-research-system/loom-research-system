DEVELOPMENT · OPEN BUILD

Loom is still being built.

The research program is being developed in public. This page separates what exists today from what is under construction and what Loom is deliberately not claiming yet.

WORKING NOW

Building the foundation.

  • Core ontology and governance concepts
  • Claims, epistemic status, verification and promotion specification
  • Evidence and provenance model
  • Flint case research artifact
  • Observation Capture API milestone
  • Cryptographic provenance infrastructure

UNDER DEVELOPMENT

The enforcement layer.

  • Schema implementation and validator hardening
  • Adversarial and reproducibility tests
  • Audit governance and correction/demotion paths
  • Automated provenance and retrieval workflows
  • Public research APIs and verification queues

NOT YET CLAIMED

What Loom will not pretend is finished.

  • A fully operational autonomous research system
  • Independent external audit of the entire research stack
  • Automatic verification of source truth
  • Complete coverage of the governance cases Loom hopes to study

BUILDING PRINCIPLE

Specification → implementation → artifact → adversarial test → public description.

Every technical feature is subordinate to the research method. A tool is not evidence. A passing validator is not proof that a claim is true. The system exists to preserve, test, and expose the research record.

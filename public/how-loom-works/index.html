METHOD · IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

How Loom works.

Loom follows the chain between a decision and an outcome. The goal is not to produce a more convincing story. It is to make the mechanism inspectable.

01

What was supposed to happen?

Start with the formal decision: a law, regulation, executive action, program, budget choice, or institutional commitment. What did the decision actually require?

02

What actually happened?

Establish the observed outcome without treating the outcome as an explanation. A policy announcement is not evidence that implementation occurred.

03

Who had to make it happen?

Map the institutions, authorities, people, resources, dependencies, information flows, and timing required to move from decision to implementation.

04

Where did things diverge?

Look for mechanisms: missing authority, capacity constraints, dependency failures, contradictory incentives, information failures, timing problems, or breakdowns in self-correction.

05

How do we know?

Trace each material claim back to evidence. Preserve contradiction. Record verification. Separate an observation from a claim, and a claim from a diagnostic explanation.

THE CORE RULE

AI may propose.
Evidence must establish.
Humans may promote.

This is not a slogan about responsible AI. It is a governance rule for the research system. The evidence model, verification process, and promotion stages are designed so that generated text cannot become an established research claim merely because it sounds plausible.

06

THREE LAYERS

Readable on the surface. Rigorous underneath.

01 · PHILOSOPHY

Mechanisms over ideology. Reproducibility over authority. Explicit uncertainty over false precision.

02 · KNOWLEDGE

Cases, observations, claims, sources, evidence, verification events, and the ontology that connects them.

03 · SYSTEMS

Validators, provenance tooling, research artifacts, and automation that enforce the methodology rather than replace it.

THE PROBLEM LOOM ADDRESSES

A decision is not the same thing as implementation.

Policies are often described as though the announcement is the event. But between a decision and an outcome sit agencies, contracts, technical systems, budgets, legal authorities, people, dependencies, information, timing, and institutional capacity. Loom studies that middle layer.

04

WHY LOOM IS DIFFERENT

Not a knowledge-management vault. Not a fact-checking site.

Loom borrows useful ideas from several research traditions — primary-source investigation, open science, structured argumentation, OSINT verification, and knowledge management — but combines them around a different problem: primary empirical governance research.

That combination matters because Loom is designed to preserve the chain from source to evidence to observation to claim, while mechanically enforcing rules about provenance, contradiction, verification, and promotion.

The system is allowed to say: we don't know yet.

06

THE CORE RULE

AI may propose.
Evidence must establish.
Humans may promote.

Loom uses automation where it can improve consistency, traceability, and error detection. But generated text does not become established research simply because it sounds plausible.

The research artifacts have explicit epistemic states. Contradictions are recorded rather than smoothed over. Evidence gaps are first-class results. Publication can fail when the underlying record fails the rules.

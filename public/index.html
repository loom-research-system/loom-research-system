THE QUESTION UNDERNEATH

A decision is not the same thing as implementation.

Policies are often described as though the announcement is the event. But between a decision and an outcome sit agencies, contracts, technical systems, budgets, legal authorities, people, dependencies, information, timing, and institutional capacity. Loom studies that middle layer.

WHY LOOM IS DIFFERENT

Simple on the outside. Rigorous underneath.

Loom combines primary-source investigation, open research, structured claims, provenance, institutional reconstruction, and mechanical validation around one problem: understanding what happens between political decisions and real-world outcomes.

The interface should be accessible to anyone. The underlying research record should be inspectable by anyone who needs to challenge it.

The system is allowed to say: we don't know yet.

07

THE CORE RULE

AI may propose.
Evidence must establish.
Humans may promote.

Loom uses automation where it can improve consistency, traceability, and error detection. But generated text does not become established research simply because it sounds plausible.

Contradictions are recorded rather than smoothed over. Evidence gaps are first-class results. Publication can fail when the underlying record fails the rules.

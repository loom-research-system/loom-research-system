EVIDENCE · RESEARCH CREDIBILITY

Don't take Loom's word for it.

Loom is being built around a simple distinction: an AI system can help find, organize, or propose information. It cannot establish a research claim by itself.

01

THE EVIDENCE CHAIN

From source to public statement.

01Source

A document, record, dataset, interview, or other identifiable source.

02Evidence

A retrieved passage, observation, or source object that can be located and inspected.

03Claim

A bounded statement about what the evidence establishes. Observation and claim are not interchangeable.

04Verification

Checks that establish whether the evidence supports the claim, including cross-verification and contradiction handling.

05Promotion

A human-governed decision about whether a claim may move to a stronger epistemic status.

02

EPISTEMIC DISCIPLINE

Uncertainty is part of the record.

What Loom will distinguish

  • Observation ≠ Claim
  • Source-backed evidence ≠ independently verified fact
  • Candidate claim ≠ supported claim
  • Diagnostic explanation ≠ established causal proof
  • Repository status ≠ public certainty

What Loom is building

The claims and verification specification uses typed epistemic statuses, retrieval requirements, cross-verification, contradiction records, near-duplicate detection, append-only verification events, and human promotion gates.

The research system is designed to make it possible to answer a basic question: why does Loom believe this?

03

PUBLIC RECORD

The technical layer is available when you want it.

The public site is intentionally not a Git repository or an Obsidian vault. Technical provenance is progressively disclosed: first the finding, then the evidence, then the research record behind the finding. The underlying repository remains open so that researchers and technical collaborators can inspect the machinery.

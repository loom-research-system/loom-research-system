ASK LOOM · PUBLIC RESEARCH STARTING POINT

Have a question about
what government is actually doing?

Ask the question in ordinary language. Loom classifies the inquiry, searches the published evidence layer, and gives you the simplest answer that the evidence can support.

Quick Read uses only claims that have already passed Loom's publication gate. It will not invent an answer when the evidence layer cannot support one.

01

START WHERE YOU ARE

You do not need to know how Loom works.

Your question becomes an Inquiry. Loom classifies what kind of research it requires, then routes it to the appropriate evidence surface.

The public interface is deliberately simpler than the machinery underneath it. If the evidence is incomplete, the answer says so. If the question needs deeper work, you can escalate it rather than treating a thin answer as a conclusion.

02

THREE LEVELS OF DEPTH

Same evidence standard. Different depth.

01 · QUICK READ

The answer first.

A plain-language response compiled from promoted claims, with evidence status, key facts, and uncertainty.

02 · LOOM BRIEF

Show me the work.

A structured reconstruction of the decision, responsible actors, implementation, evidence, contradictions, and unresolved questions.

03 · FULL LOOM AUDIT

Investigate it.

A formal research request for deeper reconstruction, source verification, evidence-gap work, competing explanations, and an accountability record.

03

ACCOUNTABILITY

The point is not to score politicians. It is to trace responsibility.

What was promised? → What authority existed? → What was required? → What actually happened? → Where did implementation diverge? → Who could act? → What happened next?

Loom can examine legislation, executive actions, public promises, agency decisions, spending, programs, regulations, and implementation—not merely whether a statement was technically true.

04

THE RULE UNDERNEATH

AI may propose.
Evidence must establish.
Humans may promote.

A Quick Read is not a lower standard of evidence. It is a smaller research surface. The browser can read published claims; it cannot promote a claim or manufacture a factual answer.

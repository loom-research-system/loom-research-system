ASK LOOM · PUBLIC RESEARCH STARTING POINT

Have a question about
what government is actually doing?

Start with a simple question. Loom is designed to give you a plain-language answer first, then let you follow the evidence as far as you need.

The public query interface is being built. The research standard is already defined; live question answering will be connected to the validated Loom evidence layer.

01

START WHERE YOU ARE

You do not need to know how Loom works.

You can ask Loom the question the way you would ask a person. The system should determine what kind of research is needed, identify the relevant public record, and give you the simplest useful answer it can support.

It should never manufacture certainty just to answer quickly. If the evidence is incomplete, the answer should tell you that—and show you what is missing.

02

THREE LEVELS OF DEPTH

Same evidence standard. Different depth.

01 · QUICK READ

The answer first.

A plain-language response, evidence status, key facts, and the most important uncertainty.

02 · LOOM BRIEF

Show me the work.

A structured reconstruction of the decision, responsible actors, implementation, evidence, contradictions, and unresolved questions.

03 · FULL LOOM AUDIT

Investigate it.

A formal research request for deeper reconstruction, source verification, evidence-gap work, competing explanations, and an accountability record.

03

ACCOUNTABILITY

The point is not to score politicians. It is to trace responsibility.

What was promised? → What authority existed? → What was required? → What actually happened? → Where did implementation diverge? → Who could act? → What happened next?

Loom can therefore examine legislation, executive actions, public promises, agency decisions, spending, programs, regulations, and implementation—not merely whether a statement was technically true.

04

THE RULE UNDERNEATH

AI may propose.
Evidence must establish.
Humans may promote.

The public interface can be simple because the research machinery underneath it is not. A Quick Read is not a lower standard of evidence. It is a smaller research surface.

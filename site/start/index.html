A five-minute introduction

So, what is Project Loom?

Project Loom is a research program about the machinery of governance: the systems that turn public decisions into things that actually happen.

The missing middle

A policy can be clear and still fail. A program can be funded and still stall.

What Loom examines

Who had authority? What depended on what? What capacity existed? Where did information move? Where could the system correct itself—and where could it not?

Three ideas

Concept 01

Governance capacity

The practical ability of institutions to turn decisions into sustained action.

Concept 02

Implementation debt

The gap between what a policy requires and what delivery institutions can sustain.

Concept 03

Dependency chains

The actors, systems, authorities, funding, contracts, and approvals a policy depends upon.

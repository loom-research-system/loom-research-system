Concepts & Ontology

Loom uses a deliberately small vocabulary for describing governance and implementation.

CON-001

Policy

A formal decision, directive, rule, or program intent.

CON-002

Governance

The institutional arrangements through which a policy is directed and carried out.

CON-003

Implementation

The process by which formal decisions become operational activity.

CON-004

Governance Capacity

The practical ability of institutions to sustain the actions a policy requires.

CON-005

Implementation Debt

The accumulated gap between policy requirements and available delivery capacity.

CON-006

Dependency Chains

The connected authorities, systems, actors, resources, and conditions required for implementation.

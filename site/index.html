The basic idea

There is machinery between a decision and an outcome.

Policy tells us what government intends. Implementation tells us what happened. Loom concentrates on the governance machinery connecting the two.

PolicyWhat was decided?
→
GovernanceWho must act, and how?

ImplementationWhat actually happens?
